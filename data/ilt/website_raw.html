ABOUT US

Innovative Lighting Technologies

Since 2003, Innovative Lighting has been at the forefront of LED innovation, transforming spaces with cutting-edge lighting solutions. With roots in Foshan, China, and a state-of-the-art facility in Vietnam since 2019, we offer unmatched manufacturing flexibility between two powerhouse locations. Our world-class technical team ensures products that exceed North American standards for quality and performance.

Sustainable Lighting

WWe craft energy-efficient solutions designed to power the future. Committed to sustainability, our products reduce energy consumption while delivering sleek, modern designs that elevate environments-from multi-family residential projects to custom designs-with a focus on energy conservation and environmental stewardship.

Why Choose Us

As a trusted OEM and ODM partner, we specialize in energy-saving fixtures, advanced LED modules, high-performance LED drivers, and latest DOB technology. We also provide stylish, energy-efficient ceiling fans, brightening and cooling spaces from homes to multi-family developments, ensuring bright futures for customers and the planet.

Vertically Integrated Facilities

Our vertically integrated facilities deliver precision and efficiency, covering metal processing, plastic forming, fixture assembly, LED drivers, DOBs, modules, and ceiling fan production. Controlling every step guarantees unbeatable quality and cost-efficiency, making us the go-to partner for price-sensitive markets.

Innovation & R&D

Our relentless R&D team pushes boundaries, developing groundbreaking products and collaborating with clients to bring visions to life. Partnering with us means working with a team committed to staying ahead in lighting and fan technology.

Certified Quality

Our comprehensive LED lineup-including under-cabinet fixtures, ceiling lights, vanity fixtures, wall sconces, outdoor lighting, and ceiling fans-is both beautiful and safe. ISO 9001 certification and multiple safety approvals (UL, cUL, ETL, CE, Energy Star, FCC, SAA) ensure your lighting and cooling needs are expertly handled.
